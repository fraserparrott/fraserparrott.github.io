This is an interactive Tableau dashboard I built during my Data Science Infinity course. It was designed to answer two simple questions: where do the most powerful earthquakes occur, and where do earthquakes happen most often?

---

## What the dashboard shows

- **Top 10 Largest Earthquakes:** a ranked table of the biggest events, with their location.
- **Earthquake Map:** every recorded event plotted on a world map, so clusters along the major fault zones are easy to see.
- **% Earthquakes by Location (Broad):** a filter and breakdown by broad region, such as North America, Asia, Oceania and South America.
- **Location Analysis:** frequency, average magnitude and maximum magnitude for each location side by side.
- **Analysis Date slider:** narrows everything to a chosen period.

---

## What stands out

In the location analysis, Puerto Rico has the most recorded earthquakes (386), followed by Hawaii (348) and Alaska (270). The map shows the events concentrated in lines rather than spread evenly, which matches the tectonic plate boundaries.

---

The dashboard is interactive. Click a region, or move the date slider, and the other views update.
